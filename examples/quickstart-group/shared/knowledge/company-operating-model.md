# Company Operating Model

The system represents one private AI group. The group owns governance, shared policies, task continuity, model access and audit rules. Subsidiaries own business domains. Departments own bounded capabilities and workflows.

Human instructions enter through the CEO. The CEO decomposes and routes work. Departments execute through registered adapters, return evidence and artifacts, and never report directly to the human. The CEO verifies and aggregates results before producing a human-readable report.

Models are replaceable engines. GroupPack data, policies, skills, evidence, artifacts, events and checkpoints remain portable and provider-independent.
