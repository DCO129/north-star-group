# Group Execution Policy

1. The CEO is the only human command and reporting gateway.
2. The scheduler may route only to registered departments with the requested capability.
3. Department execution requires a registered executor and explicit acceptance criteria.
4. Internal dispatch and return traffic is stored as compact Group IR.
5. Evidence and artifacts are content-addressed inside the GroupPack.
6. Checkpoints and task events are provider-neutral and use Asia/Shanghai timestamps.
7. Template executors are deterministic scaffolding, not a claim of model reasoning or external research.
8. Failed verification blocks aggregation and human reporting.
